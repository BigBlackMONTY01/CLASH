9. Typing indicator — show "opponent is typing..." while the other player is writing their argument
10. Rank decay — inactive players' MMR slowly drops, keeping the leaderboard competitive
11. Card reveal animation — rarity reveal with a flip/glow effect instead of instantly appearing
12. Card showcase on profile — a dedicated section showing a player's rarest/best cards
13. Cards tied to specific topics — a "Free Will" card looks different from a "Capitalism" card; topic-themed art
14. Card trading — let players gift or trade duplicates
15. Legendary card requirement — Legendary should require a verifiable S-rank performance, not random chance

19. Topic history — show that a topic has been debated 847 times globally, with the win rate per side
20. Custom topic input — let users type any topic, not just from the curated list
21. Push notifications — "Someone challenged you to a 1v1", "Your rank changed", "New daily topic"
